I secure the physical data that drives modern exploration.

A predictive model is only as reliable as the rocks it is built on. I bridge the gap between site operations and algorithmic targeting by protecting raw data fidelity before it ever touches software.

Algorithms do not care about the rain. During a high volume site investigation in Victoria, the drill crew pushed hard to meet their meterage targets. The core was fractured and the site conditions were deteriorating. In high velocity environments, the temptation is always to match the drillers' pace at the expense of accuracy.

I chose to enforce NATA compliant QA/QC protocols instead. I recalibrated the PIDs and verified the lithological boundaries before a single core box was shipped to the lab.

That experience at CDR Geotechnical shifted how I view geological data. As the mining industry races toward AI driven exploration, companies are pouring millions into complex neural networks. A machine learning model ingests field data exactly as it is collected. A mislabeled soil sample or a contaminated blank at the rig translates directly into a flawed predictive model months later.

Building downstream 3D models taught me exactly how vulnerable software is to poor field execution.

My academic background forced me to look at both sides of this equation. During my Master of Geoscience at the University of Melbourne, I spent weeks in Bermagui mapping poly deformed terranes by hand. I also studied the structural vulnerabilities of the data pipelines we use to interpret those maps. Building spatial models in Leapfrog Geo and ArcGIS made it entirely clear that you cannot fix bad sampling with good software.

01 / Exploration Software Suite

I needed a tangible way to flag compromised data before it infected an exploration pipeline. I built a suite of logic engines operating independently of easily distorted metrics.

Geochemical assay data is heavily skewed. If you use standard deviation to search for anomalies in parts per billion data, extreme outliers distort the mean and blind you to subtle mineralization patterns.

Strata is a client side web application that calculates Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) statistics in real time. It allows geologists to drop raw CSV assay files directly into the browser, apply log transformations instantly, and map severe anomalies securely without relying on an external data science team.

Geochemistry is only one variable. If downhole survey data is compromised, the resulting spatial model is fundamentally flawed. I wrote DrillCheck to isolate gyro deviations and magnetic interference. This prevents corrupted spatial coordinates from entering the database.

Coordinates alone do not find deposits. You need to understand the structural controls. I developed Vein3D to model and render complex geological vein networks dynamically. It allows exploration teams to visualize structural geometry directly from raw intercepts.

The final step is synthesizing these inputs. TargetGrid handles multi layer spatial raster blending and proximity buffering. It processes multi criteria spatial data to generate prospectivity heatmaps.

DrillCheck

Identifies gyro deviation and magnetic interference in drill surveys to prevent spatial database corruption.
TargetGrid

This is what I bring to an exploration team. I am comfortable owning the physical site realities, from managing the chain of custody to calibrating instruments and executing structural mapping.
